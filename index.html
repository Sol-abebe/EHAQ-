Three Core Elements of Quality

Integrated Juran Quality Trilogy Framework used in the Ethiopian Strategy

1. Quality Planning

Brings systems thinking to high-level governance. Designs health structures that deliver the right care at the right time, allocating resources based on measured population health needs.

2. Quality Improvement

Continuous iterative testing (PDSA cycles & Kaizen) to achieve ambitious aims. Combines frontline Kaizen "engine" with Model for Improvement structure for rapid scale-up.

3. Quality Control

Normative assurance process enforcing standards, clinical audits, FMHACA regulations, and EQA to maintain performance and eliminate avoidable patient harm.

Key Current System Challenges

Critical bottlenecks identified during WHO diagnostic phase

  • Neonatal Mortality Lag: Slow reduction rate in neonatal deaths compared to overall under-five mortality gains.
  • Inconsistent Clinical Standards: Disconnect between FMHACA regulatory standards and facility capacity to comply.
  • Data Feedback Disconnect: HMIS data reporting lacks real-time downstream feedback loops to primary health facilities.
  • Supply & Maintenance Gaps: Frequent stock-outs of critical reagents and unmaintained medical equipment.
Strategic goal: Bridge these baseline gaps by 2020 via standardized Ql structures.

Execution Commitments & Roadmap

Institutional structural shifts and 5-year implementation timeline

1

Formal Quality Directorate

Establish a dedicated FMoH Quality Unit (Directorate equivalent) supported by a multi-disciplinary Quality Steering Committee to oversee planning, QI, and control nationwide.

2

Quality Champions Network

Capacitate dedicated quality leads across all FMoH Directorates, FMHACA, PFSA, EHIA, professional associations, and Regional Core Processes as trained quality experts.

3

Facility & Community QI Units

Institute functional Quality Teams within all public and private health facilities and transform the Health Development Army (HDA) into community-level QI teams.

5-Year Strategic Implementation Timeline (2016–2020)

Short-Term Phase (Years 1–2)

Foundations & Standards Harmonization

• Refine NQS & ratify national implementation roadmap.
• Harmonize FMoH/MSD and FMHACA hospital regulatory standards.
• Formally establish Quality Directorate & launch annual Quality Summits.
• Draft Patient Rights Charter & launch quality guidelines for EHIA insurance empanelment.

Medium-Term Phase (Years 2–4)

Enforcement, Insurance Linkage & Scale-Up

• Full enforcement of FMHACA standards & WHO benchmark guidelines.
• Roll out quality-based performance agreements for insurance empanelment.
• Expand EHAQ network beyond hospitals to primary health units & private facilities.
• Institutionalize local level data utilization & staff satisfaction initiatives.

Long-Term Phase (Years 4–5)

Sustainability & System Transformation

• Institutionalize continuous Quality Planning, QI, and Quality Control across all levels.
• Fully activate Community Endorsement Groups nationwide.
• Integrate insurance claims data analytics to drive system-wide clinical improvement.
• Secure lower-middle-income country baseline health outcomes.
